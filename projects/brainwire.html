BrainWire End-to-End Business Intelligence Dashboard (Power BI)

View Full Project on GitHub
Power BI DAX Power Query Python

Complete Business Intelligence solution for BrainWire, an imaginary tech-product e-commerce company. The dataset was synthetically generated in Python with realistic transactional, customer, marketing and web behavior patterns. Built in Power BI using a star schema, DAX measures, KPI tracking and multi-page executive dashboards.

Problem

The business lacked a unified reporting layer to monitor revenue performance, customer value, marketing efficiency, product profitability and return behavior in a single structured decision-making environment.

Approach

  • Designed and generated a realistic multi-table dataset in Python representing orders, returns, campaigns, customers and web behavior.
  • Built a clean star-schema data model in Power BI with fact and dimension tables.
  • Developed DAX measures for revenue, CLV, ROI, return rate, RFM scoring and engagement KPIs.
  • Created multi-page executive dashboards with drill-through, slicers and cross-filtering for interactive analysis.

Key Insights

  • Revenue peaked in 2021 before declining in 2023, indicating demand fluctuation and potential market saturation effects.
  • A small group of products drives a disproportionate share of revenue, highlighting dependency risk and pricing leverage opportunities.
  • Marketing ROI varies significantly by channel and month, showing uneven campaign efficiency.
  • High CLV segments (31–50 age group) demonstrate strong retention potential.
  • Return patterns suggest dissatisfaction and product defects as primary drivers revealing quality and expectation gaps.

Data Model

Star-schema architecture with fact and dimension tables.

  • Fact: Orders, Returns, CampaignResponses, WebBehavior
  • Dimensions: Customers, Products, Campaigns, DateTable
