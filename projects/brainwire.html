BrainWire – End-to-End Business Intelligence Dashboard (Power BI)

View Full Project on GitHub

Complete Business Intelligence solution for BrainWire, an imaginary tech-product e-commerce company. Dataset was synthetically generated in Python with realistic transactional, customer, marketing, and web behavior patterns. Built in Power BI using star schema, DAX measures, KPI tracking, and multi-page executive dashboards.

Data Model

Star-schema architecture with fact and dimension tables.

  • Fact: Orders, Returns, CampaignResponses, WebBehavior
  • Dimensions: Customers, Products, Campaigns, DateTable

Key Insights

  • Revenue peaked in 2021 before declining in 2023, indicating demand fluctuation.
  • A small set of products drives a large share of total revenue.
  • Return rate is low overall but varies by category and product.
  • Marketing ROI fluctuates monthly, showing uneven campaign efficiency.
  • Bounce rate remains elevated across channels, suggesting UX opportunities.
  • RFM/CLV segments show strong retention potential in ages 31–50.
  • Returns are driven mainly by dissatisfaction and defects.
